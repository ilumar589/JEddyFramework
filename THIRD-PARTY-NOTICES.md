## Native libraries

Built from pinned sources by `natives/build.py` (`natives/versions.json`) and shipped in the
`jeddy-natives-<platform>` jars and in packaged images (`runtime/lib/jeddy/natives`). Each
library's license file travels with it in `natives/<platform>/licenses/`.
